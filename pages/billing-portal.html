Invoice Lookup

Enter the invoice number printed on the patient's billing statement to retrieve its current status and summary.

Billing Guidelines

Invoice numbers follow the hospital's standard format:

INV-<YEAR>-<NUMBER> e.g. INV-2024-0912

Billing staff should verify the patient's identity before discussing any invoice over the phone. Invoices marked CONFIDENTIAL require supervisor approval to disclose.
